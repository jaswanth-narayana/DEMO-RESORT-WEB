Gastronomy

Chef Nedumaran's Kitchen

Indulge in a 7-course seasonal tasting menu highlighting wild-foraged mushrooms, organic vegetables from our garden, and local meats. Chef Nedumaran personally cooks for our guests, bringing exceptional culinary storytelling to your table.

Wellness

Geothermal Thermal Springs

Heal your body and soul in our natural volcanic hot springs. Carved out of mountain basalt rocks, the mineral-rich waters sit at a constant 39°C, offering therapeutic relaxation under a snowy or starry sky. Therapeutic massages are available daily.
